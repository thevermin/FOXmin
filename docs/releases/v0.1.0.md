## FOXmin 0.1.0

The first version of FOXmin: click any window on your Mac and it just works, on the first click, like on Windows.

### What it does
- **One-click windows**: a click on a background window activates it and does what you clicked (buttons, links, text fields, tabs, title bars).
- **Drag in one move**: drag a background window, or select text in it, without clicking it first.
- **Right and middle click too**: each one can be turned on or off from the menu.
- Focus keeps working like it always does on macOS, so new windows and dialogs (like the Touch ID prompt) still get focus wherever your mouse is.
- It never touches the menu bar, the Dock, open menus, popovers, the desktop or floating panels.
- In English and Spanish: FOXmin follows your Mac's language.

### Menu bar
- **Click** the icon to turn FOXmin on or off. The icon is filled when it's on and just an outline when it's off.
- **Right-click** for the menu:
  - **Excluded apps**: exclude the app in front with one click, or add any app with **Add app…**.
  - **Activation speed**: Fast, Normal or Patient (for slow apps, often Electron ones).
  - **Open at login**, **About FOXmin** and **Check for updates…** (it also checks by itself once a day).

### Install
1. Download `FOXmin-0.1.0.zip`, double-click it and drag **FOXmin** to **Applications**.
2. The first time, **right-click → Open** (macOS warns because the app isn't from the App Store). If it still won't open, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Give it the **Accessibility** permission when macOS asks.

If you use a *focus follows mouse* or *auto raise* app, turn it off: FOXmin doesn't need it.

Requires macOS 13 or newer.
